Sources

For the notes, the texts, and the plates

Each note names the work where its claim can be checked; click a note in the reader to see it. This page gathers them in one place. Every cited entry has been opened and compared with its note by Claude, an AI, and notes were cut back to what their sources say. Notes stay drafts until a person checks them too.

Texts

  • Joseph Conrad, Lord Jim (1900). Project Gutenberg eBook #5658, gutenberg.org/ebooks/5658. Public domain. Wording, spelling, and punctuation are unchanged; only the Gutenberg licence header and footer are removed.
  • Joseph Conrad, Heart of Darkness (1899; book form 1902). Project Gutenberg eBook #219, gutenberg.org/ebooks/219. Public domain. Split into its three parts; wording, spelling, and punctuation are unchanged, and only the Gutenberg licence header and footer are removed.
  • Leo Tolstoy, The Death of Ivan Ilyich (1886), translated by Constance Garnett (New York: John Lane, 1915; she spells the name “Ilyitch”). Project Gutenberg eBook #79990, gutenberg.org/ebooks/79990. Public domain. Split into its twelve chapters. Wording and spelling are unchanged; double hyphens are set as true dashes, the first word of chapter 1 is set in ordinary capitals, and the Gutenberg licence header and footer, the title, and the date line are removed.
  • Herman Melville, Bartleby, the Scrivener: A Story of Wall-Street (1853). Project Gutenberg eBook #11231, gutenberg.org/ebooks/11231. Public domain. Divided into six parts at scene breaks; wording, spelling, and punctuation are unchanged, and only the Gutenberg licence header and footer, the title, and the byline are removed.
  • Hermann Hesse, Siddhartha (1922), in English. Project Gutenberg eBook #2500, gutenberg.org/ebooks/2500. The Gutenberg file does not name the translator. Split into its twelve chapters (the two parts and the dedication are not shown); wording, spelling, and punctuation are unchanged, and only the Gutenberg licence header and footer are removed.
  • Herman Melville, Billy Budd, Sailor (An Inside Narrative) (written 1888 to 1891, first published 1924). The Constable and Company edition (London, 1924), Penn State Electronic Classics copy, archive.org/details/melville_herman_1819_1891_billy_budd. Public domain. Line-end hyphens were rejoined and the thirty chapters split into files; the later scholarly text (Hayford and Sealts, 1962) is not used.
  • Francis Turner Palgrave, ed., The Golden Treasury of the Best Songs and Lyrical Poems in the English Language (1861), Fourth Book (poems 166 to 288). Project Gutenberg eBook #19221, gutenberg.org/ebooks/19221. Public domain. The poems are regrouped by poet and kept in Palgrave’s numbering and his titles (set in title case); apostrophes and quotation marks are set curly and double hyphens as dashes; his author lines, notes, and the other three books are not included.
