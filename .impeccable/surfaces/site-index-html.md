# Surface brief: Scholia home (library cover)

Scope: site/index.html. Visitor mode: Persuade (the library's front door).
Audience: curious adults and students arriving cold. Action: open a book and start reading. Proof: the notes themselves and the open licence. Constraint from the owner: nothing on the home page may belong to one book; the library will grow.
Memorable moment: a frontispiece plate (Spitzweg's The Bookworm in a buff mount) facing a title page whose contents list is the way in.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is the library's opening spread: a frontispiece facing a title page, as in a Victorian illustrated book. It refuses both the full-bleed hero banner and the buff band that butts into the page; buff appears only as the mount of each plate, so there is no seam.

OWN-WORLD: Inherits DESIGN.md: newsprint ground, black ink, buff wrapper (#d6c69f) now used as plate mounts and the logo field, Old Standard TT, thick-thin rules, dotted leaders, run-in capital heads, square corners. New: the S-with-asterisk logo; captioned plates numbered Plate I to III.

STORY: The visitor sees a beautiful, bookish object, reads one line about what Scholia does, finds the library's contents, and opens a book; further down, plates accompany how the notes work and how to help.
